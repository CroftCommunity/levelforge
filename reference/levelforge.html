EMOJI WARS — HOW IT WORKS

The idea. Build a level, fling your hero emoji at the villains. Villains are emoji stamped in target material — break them all to clear the level.
Placing. Arm a shape in the tray, tap the board to stamp it in the current material. 🖌 paint is different: drag to color in a freeform blob — it becomes one solid piece.
Adjusting (✋). Tap a piece to select. Drag to move — it lifts above your finger and edges magnet-snap flush (blobs skip the magnet). Pinch resizes. Twist or ⟳ rotates. The arrow pad nudges one grid square per tap.
Looking (🔍). Toggle 🔍, then pinch to zoom up to 4×, drag to pan, double-tap to reset.
Materials. wood breaks, stone barely breaks, metal never breaks, ice is slippery and melts during Test, rubber bounces, target is fragile and is the thing to destroy.
Backdrops. Pick scenery, or 🖼 upload your own 1600×900 image. 📋 copies a ready-made brief for an image agent — add your style notes, generate, import. A faint alignment grid overlays while editing.
Behaviors. ⚓ anchors a piece. ↔ gives it a patrol path. ✎ attaches a note — your intent in words, which travels with the level.
Test (▶). Gravity exists only here. Drag the hero back from the launcher, release to fire. ■ returns to editing, level untouched.
The schema ({ }). The level is JSON. Copy it into a Claude chat; paste Claude's levels back and Load. 💾 saves named versions grouped into scenes.
