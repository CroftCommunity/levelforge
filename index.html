EMOJI WARS — HOW IT WORKS

The idea. Build a level, then play it in one of three modes. In slingshot (the default) you fling your hero emoji at the villains — emoji stamped in target material — and break them all to clear the level.
Placing. Arm a shape in the tray, tap the board to stamp it in the current material. 🖌 paint is different: drag to color in a freeform blob — it becomes one solid piece.
Adjusting (✋). Tap a piece to select. Drag to move — it lifts above your finger and edges magnet-snap flush (blobs skip the magnet). Pinch resizes. Drag a corner grab of the dashed guide box to free-rotate — it soft-snaps to 15° so decline ramps are easy to dial in. Twist or ⟳ rotates too. The arrow pad nudges one grid square per tap.
Looking (🔍). Toggle 🔍, then pinch to zoom up to 4×, drag to pan, double-tap to reset.
MATERIALS — the swatch row picks the material a new piece is stamped in (and re-materials the selected piece). Material carries all the physics; shape is separate.
  • wood — light; breaks under a firm hit.
  • stone — heavy; barely breaks (needs a hard hit).
  • metal — heaviest; never breaks.
  • ice — slippery, and melts away during Test.
  • rubber — bouncy; never breaks.
  • target — fragile green; this is the villain material — the thing to destroy. Emoji stamped in target are your villains.
THE SELECTED PIECE — tap a piece to select it; its controls appear in the inspector.
  • ⧉ copy — duplicates the piece right next to itself, carrying every attribute with it (material, angle, size, ⚓ anchor, ↔ path, ✎ note, 🔗 weld, 🖼️ sprite, 🛡️ role, and its ✨ hit effect). The copy is selected so you can drag it into place.
  • ✕ remove — deletes the piece (undo brings it back).
  • ⟳ rotate — turns the piece 90° clockwise.
  • ⚓ anchor — pins the piece in place; it becomes immovable static geometry in Test (walls, ramps, ledges).
  • ↔ moving platform — gives the piece a patrol path (drag the ↔ handle to set the far end; it ping-pongs between the two points). Movers matter most in drop & drive.
  • ✎ note — attaches a note: your intent in words. It rides inside the schema so it travels with the level to Claude and back. 🎤 can dictate it.
  • 🔗 weld — fuses this piece to the piece you selected just before it, so they move as one rigid body in Test. Tap one piece, then another, then 🔗. Tap 🔗 again to unweld. (A painted blob is already one welded piece.)
  • 🖼️ sprite (emoji only) — skins the emoji with your own uploaded image instead of the glyph. Tap again to remove and go back to the glyph.
  • 🛡️ shield / protect (target only) — turns a villain into a hostage: a piece you must not destroy. If any protected target breaks, the level fails. Use it to force careful aim. Off = an ordinary destroy-me villain.
  • ✨ hit effect (emoji only) — picks what the emoji does the moment it's destroyed in Test (see EFFECTS below). Tap to open the picker.
  • 🏁 goal / flag — marks any piece as the goal zone: the catch tray to land in for drop, the flag to reach for drive. Tap again to clear it.
The readout under the controls shows the selected piece's position, size, and angle. The arrow pad in the corner nudges it one grid square per tap.
EFFECTS (✨) — a hit effect fires the instant an emoji is destroyed in Test (when your hero smashes it). Purely for flair, except explode, which also shoves and chain-detonates neighbours. A copied piece keeps its effect, so you can build a whole cluster of poppers or bombs from one.
  • 🫧 pop — vanishes in a quick bubble-pop puff of light specks.
  • 💥 explode — a blast that shoves nearby pieces and detonates breakable neighbours; chain reactions emerge. 💣 / 🧨 use this by default.
  • 🧩 break apart — shatters into tumbling shards (in its material colour) that fall and fade.
  • 💧 splash — bursts into droplets that arc up and rain down.
  • 🎉 confetti — erupts into a shower of colourful confetti strips.
An emoji with an effect will break on a solid hit even in a material that normally never breaks, so the effect can play.
Modes (⚙ Settings). Settings cycles the mode: slingshot → drop → drive. In drop the hero spawns at the start pad and falls; tap anywhere to hop, but only while it's resting on something (or a split-second after rolling off an edge). Villains are hazards — touching one flashes red and restarts you at the top (an attempt counter ticks up; nothing is destroyed). Reach the 🏁 goal (a catch tray) to clear. Drive (Red Ball) gives you on-screen ◀ ▶ and a jump to steer the hero to the 🏁 goal; villains are lethal there too. Drop and drive never break pieces — but ice still melts.
World shape (⚙ Settings). A level's size is part of its data: wide 1600×900 or tall 900×1600 (tall suits drop and portrait play). Switching keeps every object where it sits and just flags how many pieces landed out of bounds — nudge them back in. Backdrops, the floor, zoom-to-fit and the agent brief all follow the level's shape.
Backdrops (⚙ Settings). The backdrop lives in Settings so it doesn't crowd the editor. Pick scenery, or 🖼 upload your own image (cover-fit to the level's size). 📋 copies a ready-made brief for an image agent — it states the exact dimensions and floor line for the current world shape; add your style notes, generate, import. A faint alignment grid overlays while editing.
Test (▶). Gravity exists only here, built fresh from the schema and thrown away on ■ — the level is never touched by physics. In slingshot, drag the hero back from the launcher and release to fire. In drop, tap to hop; in drive, use the on-screen controls.
The schema ({ }). The level is JSON. Copy it into a Claude chat; paste Claude's levels back and Load. 💾 saves named versions grouped into scenes, alongside committed levels.
